# Product Context

## MVP goal

Enable a small business to register, create/manage an order, receive a secure tracking link, manually share it, update order status, and let a customer view the latest status without creating an account.

## Target businesses

Restaurants, bakeries, home food businesses, Instagram/Facebook sellers, small retailers, and local delivery businesses. Nepal may be the initial market, but avoid unnecessary Nepal-specific architectural assumptions.

## Build now

Authentication; business onboarding/profile/settings; OWNER/STAFF membership; customers; products; orders; status/history; secure public tracking; basic dashboard; order search/filtering; responsive UI; manual payment status; delivery/pickup information; server authorization/validation; critical tests; seed data; basic deployment/logging/monitoring.

## Do not build

WhatsApp/Messenger/Instagram/SMS/email automation; AI; payment gateways; inventory/stock/warehouse/purchasing; advanced delivery/driver management; advanced analytics; customer tracking accounts; subscription billing; white-labeling; complex permissions.

## Primary UX

Order creation first. Public tracking second. Mobile is critical.
